# Mechana™ Trademark and Brand Policy

This policy explains how to refer to the project and use its visual identity. It
does not modify the Apache License 2.0 or provide legal advice.

## Official naming

- Official project and product name: **Mechana**.
- Use **Mechana™** on the first prominent reference in public-facing material;
  use **Mechana** thereafter.
- Do not abbreviate, pluralize, translate, or change the capitalization of the
  name in a way that suggests a different official name.

## Official logo

Use only the files in this directory. The canonical mark is a coral hexagon with
three nodes joined by two diagonal connectors and no horizontal cross-piece.

- Preserve the logo's proportions, orientation, colors, and clear space.
- Keep clear space of at least half the diameter of a lower node.
- Use the supplied dark, light, transparent, or monochrome variant that provides
  adequate contrast.
- Do not redraw, distort, rotate, crop, animate, bevel, shadow, recolor, combine,
  or apply three-dimensional effects to the logo.

## Colors and typography

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Coral | `#FF6B6F` |
| Ink | `#0D1117` |
| Muted gray | `#6B7280` |
| Light gray | `#E5E7EB` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

The preferred typeface is Sora. The wordmark uses an extra-bold weight (`850`)
with tight tracking; the tagline uses a semibold weight (`650`). Use the supplied
SVG wordmarks when exact typography matters.

## Acceptable uses

You may, subject to applicable law:

- make truthful, non-misleading references to the Mechana project or software;
- state accurately that software interoperates with or is based on Mechana;
- reproduce the unmodified name or logo when reasonably necessary to identify
  the origin of an official, unmodified Mechana distribution; and
- link to the official project using an unmodified brand asset without implying
  sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or affiliation.

## Prohibited uses

Without prior written permission from Mark Vita, do not:

- use Mechana or a confusingly similar name or logo for your project, product,
  service, organization, domain name, application name, or social-media account;
- imply endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or official status;
- use the marks in advertising, merchandise, or other commercial branding; or
- register or claim rights in the marks or confusingly similar marks.

Forks and modified distributions should use their own name and visual identity,
while describing their relationship to Mechana truthfully and without confusion.
